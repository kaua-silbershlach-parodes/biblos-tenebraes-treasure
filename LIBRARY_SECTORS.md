## Definitions

- **Entry**: one file in the library, counted once, in exactly one sector.
  The sector totals above sum to the library total.
- **Book**: an entry in a book format. Of the library's entries, **626 are
  PDFs**; the remaining entries are the supporting archive around them
  (pages, images, and catalog files).
- **Sector**: the top-level division of the library. Every entry belongs to
  one sector, and every sector has its own folder in this repository.

## Scope and restrictions

- Book names, authors, and verbatim text are never published. Only totals,
  sector structure, and derived techniques appear in this repository.
- Scanned/image PDFs do not feed the engine until they pass OCR; their
  count is reported above so the coverage gap stays visible.
- Totals are measured on the library as stored, and are restated whenever
  the library changes.
